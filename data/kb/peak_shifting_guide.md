# Shifting Flexible Household Appliances

Deferrable appliances such as washing machines, dishwashers, water heaters, and home EV chargers are ideal candidates for off-peak scheduling. Running these loads outside the 18:00-22:00 peak window helps reduce grid stress and takes advantage of lower off-peak ToD rates.
