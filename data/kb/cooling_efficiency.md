# Managing Evening Air-Conditioning Runtime

Air-conditioning is often the single largest contributor to evening peak demand between 18:00 and 22:00. Trimming unnecessary evening compressor runtime by 1 to 2 hours—for example using timers or pre-cooling before peak hours—reduces both peak-window charges and total kWh consumption.
