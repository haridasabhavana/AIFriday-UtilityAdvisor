# Illustrative Domestic Time-of-Day Tariff (TAR-ILL-001)

This illustrative residential Time-of-Day (ToD) schedule uses four hourly windows with no slabs or fixed charges:
- Off-Peak (23:00-06:00): Rs 6.00 per kWh
- Standard (06:00-18:00): Rs 7.50 per kWh
- Peak (18:00-22:00): Rs 10.20 per kWh
- Shoulder (22:00-23:00): Rs 7.50 per kWh

Shifting flexible consumption from the 18:00-22:00 peak window to the 23:00-06:00 off-peak window lowers energy cost without reducing total comfort.
