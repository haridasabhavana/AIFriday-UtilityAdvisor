# EnergyWise AI Advisory Grounding Policy

All quantitative figures (kWh, INR cost, savings, peak share, CO2 estimates, and forecasts) must be computed deterministically by the backend calculation engines. Knowledge base documents explain concepts and policies; they must never be used to invent customer-specific savings.
