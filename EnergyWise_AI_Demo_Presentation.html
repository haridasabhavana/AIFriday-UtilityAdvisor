1. Problem & Value Proposition

Bridging the Gap Between Smart Meters and Real Action

Smart meters generate billions of readings, yet consumers only see a monthly bill—and ungrounded chatbots risk hallucinated savings.

The Disconnect Today

  • Peak Blindness: Consumers miss how 18:00–22:00 peak rates (₹10.20/kWh) inflate bills.
  • Static Dashboards: Charts show history without ranking what to change or showing before/after impact.
  • AI Hallucination & PII Risk: Raw LLM prompting invents ₹ savings and leaks customer identifiers.

The EnergyWise AI Universe Promise

"EnergyWise doesn't just tell consumers how much electricity they use. It explains where savings opportunities exist, recommends what to change, demonstrates the projected impact, and helps consumers commit to an actionable plan."

  • Honest Provenance: Clearly separates Measured, Estimated, and Projected.
  • Verifiable AI: Every number in chat is checked against deterministic tool outputs.

2. Option B Architecture Constellation

Six Connected Layers Working in Harmony

Every layer has a strict boundary—UI and API orchestrate, engines compute, MCP connects, and AI explains.

1. Experience Layer

  • Next.js 15 + Tailwind (port 3000)
  • FastAPI (port 8000)
  • CSV upload (ts/kwh & aliases) with raw preservation.
  • Dashboard & simulator call engines directly.

2. Engine & MCP Orbit

  • Deterministic Engines: TAR-ILL-001 billing, recommendations, what-if simulator, forecast, CO₂.
  • FastMCP Stdio: engines_server, kb_server, smart_meter_server.
  • Action Plan Store: Persisted per cust_ref in SQLite.

3. Trust & Grounding Shield

  • PII Vault: data/pii_vault.db isolated from AI & MCP; downstream uses cust_ref.
  • privacy.scan(): Redacts/blocks PII before any gateway call.
  • grounding.check(): Number-level check + automatic Rules Mode fallback.
