1. Business Problem & USP

Why Smart-Meter Data Alone Doesn't Change Behaviour

Utilities collect hourly kWh data, yet customers only see a total monthly bill—and raw LLM chatbots risk hallucinated savings and privacy leaks.

Customer & Utility Challenges

  • Opaque Bills: Customers don't see how 18:00–22:00 peak rates (₹10.20/kWh) drive their bill.
  • No Actionable Path: Static charts don't rank what to change or show before/after savings.
  • AI Trust & Privacy Risk: Letting an LLM compute ₹ savings causes hallucinations and PII exposure.

EnergyWise USP Proposition

"EnergyWise doesn't just tell consumers how much electricity they use. It explains where savings opportunities exist, recommends what to change, demonstrates the projected impact, and helps consumers commit to an actionable plan."

  • Clear Evidence: Separates Measured, Estimated, and Projected values.
  • Zero Hallucination: Every number in AI replies is verified against engine tool outputs.

2. Option B Architecture

Deterministic Engines + Tool-Calling Agent Over MCP

Clean separation of UI, API, deterministic calculation engines, MCP tool servers, privacy vault, and grounded AI.

Presentation & API

  • Next.js 15 + Tailwind (port 3000)
  • FastAPI (port 8000)
  • Dashboard & simulator call deterministic engines directly (zero LLM latency).
  • CSV upload (ts/kwh & aliases) with raw preservation.

Engines & FastMCP

  • Deterministic Engines: TAR-ILL-001 billing, recommendations, what-if simulator, forecast, CO₂ (0.82 kg/kWh).
  • FastMCP Stdio Servers: engines_server, kb_server, smart_meter_server.
  • My Energy Action Plan: Persisted per cust_ref in SQLite.

Privacy, Grounding & Fallback

  • PII Vault: data/pii_vault.db isolated from AI & MCP servers; downstream uses cust_ref (C001…).
  • privacy.scan(): Redacts/blocks PII before LLM calls.
  • grounding.check(): Number-level check; automatic Rules Mode fallback if gateway is offline.
No custom AI training • Synthetic data only • Audit logged in data/energywise.db Slide 3 / 6
